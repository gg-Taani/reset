RE·SET

Weekly Meal Plan

Pure-vegetarian · high-protein · no egg  |  cook for the 12 PM – 8 PM window only · phulka = roti without oil
Monday
Lunch
Kala chana curry (1½ katori) + 2 phulka + baingan bharta (low oil) + kachumber salad + 1 bowl curd
4 PM
Roasted makhana (1 cup) + masala chaas
Dinner
Paneer bhurji with capsicum-peas (100 g paneer) + 1 ragi roti + cucumber-tomato salad
Tuesday
Lunch
Lobia masala (1½ katori) + 2 jowar roti + gobhi-matar sabzi + salad + curd
4 PM
Hung curd bowl + 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds
Dinner
Soya keema matar (50 g dry soya) + 1 phulka + big salad
Wednesday
Lunch
Chole (1½ katori) + 1 katori rice + bhindi sabzi (low oil) + onion-cucumber salad + curd
4 PM
1 guava or orange + 6–8 almonds
Dinner
Tofu bhurji (150 g tofu) + 1 phulka + tomato soup (no cream)
Thursday
Lunch
Panchmel dal (1½ katori) + 2 phulka + methi-gajar sabzi + salad + curd
4 PM
Sprouts bhel (moong sprouts, onion, tomato, lemon, no sev) + green tea
Dinner
Hariyali paneer tikka (100 g, grilled) + sautéed beans-carrot + 1 phulka
Friday
Lunch
Kadhi with soya nuggets (no pakora, 1½ katori) + 1 katori rice + cabbage-peas sabzi + salad
4 PM
Roasted chana (small handful) + green tea
Dinner
Mushroom-tofu matar curry (100 g tofu) + 1 jowar roti + salad
Saturday
Lunch
Rajma (1½ katori) + 2 phulka + beans-gajar sabzi + kachumber salad + curd
4 PM
Paneer cubes (60 g) with chaat masala + cucumber sticks
Dinner
Moong dal-oats vegetable khichdi (1½ katori) + 1 bowl curd + big salad
Sunday
Lunch
Palak paneer (100 g paneer) + 2 phulka + onion salad + cucumber raita
4 PM
Tomato-cucumber-peanut salad (1 tbsp peanuts) + masala chaas
Dinner
Masoor dal-vegetable soup (big bowl) + sprouts tikki (2, pan-grilled) + green salad
Every day
• Half the plate = salad & vegetables
• No sugar in chai · no biscuits / namkeen
• ~100 g protein a day (dal, paneer, soya, curd)
• Drink 2.5–3 litres of water
• Nothing to eat before 12 noon or after 8 PM
Goal: 78 kg → 70 kg  ·  prepared with the RESET plan
